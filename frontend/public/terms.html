What this is

theclockisticking.xyz is a web page that shows the state of a game account on the Miden network and helps you build transactions for it with your own wallet. The game is an open-source homage to Fomo3D: players buy keys at a rising price, each accepted key extends a countdown, and when the countdown ends the whole pot is paid to the account that bought the last accepted key. The next round opens at once in the same account.

The page is published for demonstration and entertainment. It is provided as is, without warranty of any kind.

Who runs it

Nobody operates the game. The game account is an immutable network account on Miden: once deployed, no one, including the people who wrote it, can change its rules, pause it, or withdraw from it. The page's publisher does not hold, custody, or route any player funds and takes no fee or share of any pot. The contract pays its own network fees from a small reserve anyone may top up.
